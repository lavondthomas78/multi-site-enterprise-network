# LinkedIn post draft

I completed a networking milestone for Royal Technology Solutions: two separate Cisco Packet Tracer designs, integrated into one working multi-site lab.

The first design connects a campus and branch. The second models an Enterprise campus with an OSPF core, departmental data and voice VLANs, and redundant gateways. The third brings them together with a shared service path and a dedicated Enterprise firewall and edge router.

The integrated lab now supports:
• End-to-end PC connectivity across sites
• Website access in both directions
• Cross-site email, including confirmed replies
• Cross-site phone calls

The most valuable part was troubleshooting the integration: resolving overlapping addresses, preserving existing floor uplinks, distributing return routes, checking firewall policies, and following packets to locate failures.

This started as coursework and grew into something I enjoy building in my own time. Completing it gave me more practice designing, configuring, troubleshooting, and documenting networks from end to end.

I’m proud of the progress—and of having a finished project I can explain and demonstrate.

I’ll share the GitHub walkthrough with the two original designs and the integrated result once the project files are packaged.
